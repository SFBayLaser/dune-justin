  • 92 to 97% of sites with hits in all three views are covered by a true 3-hit point.

A site is a particle in a 1 cm cell. Covered: a built point uses hits from its ball and sits within 0.5 cm.

For the drift-parallel track, 94 to 97% of the sites with all three views are covered by a true 3-hit point, so the matching works where all three hits exist. Where only two views have hits, 80 to 98% are covered by 2-hit points. The missing U hit is probably the weak induction signal for a track along the drift, which is a hypothesis we have not tested. Of the normal-hit three-view sites on that track the baseline covers 73% and Fast without exclusivity 92%. Site recall is not comparable with the triple recall of 14 to 42% shown earlier, which counts every possible hit combination.
