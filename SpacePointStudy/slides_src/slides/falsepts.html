This is the breakdown of the false points. Most are not ghosts between different particles but combinations of hits from the same particle that lie in different places, for example the same track crossing the induction and collection wires at different positions. Long hits are over-represented. A Supera-style match to the nearest voxel, without checking that hits belong together, would probably count many of these as true; we have not measured that.
