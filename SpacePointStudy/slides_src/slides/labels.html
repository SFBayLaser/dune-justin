THE TRUTH LABEL

Each label we tried misleads in its own way

SAME PARTICLE

Too generous

Hits from one particle are not necessarily one deposit. About half of the pure 3-hit points were 5 cm or more from any deposit of that particle.

SHARED DEPOSIT

Biased against tilted tracks

f is applied per 0.2 cm voxel. On a track inclined in drift, 95% of nearby points have no qualifying deposit at f = 0.2, and 45% at f = 0.05.

NORMAL HITS ONLY

Blind to long hits

Points with a long hit are never true. On the drift-parallel track in event 0, none of its long-hit points counted, 0% of 500.

We need a label that asks whether the hits belong together, allowing for hits smeared along the track.

The first label, same particle only, overstated correct points. The second, sharing one 0.2 cm voxel deposit, fixed that but fails when a hit's charge comes from a long stretch of track, because no single voxel reaches f. The third problem is that the headline numbers only count normal hits, so drift-parallel tracks, which are made of long hits, were invisible. The display of event 0 and event idx 1 is how we found these.
